## Revisable engineering interpretations (E)

These interpret the source request and existing vision; they are not extra human
quotes or separately accepted promises.

- E1: Library, thin agent-friendly CLI, packaged help/manifest and provider-free deterministic capabilities.
- E2: Public Agent APIs, explicit provider choice, actionable auth errors. Binding/engine 0.22.0 is the current engineering baseline, not a permanent contract pin. Canonical IDs: openai, anthropic, gemini, openai-chatgpt, github-copilot.
- E3: Immutable exact-parent revisions, brief/scene/seed/CSD/audio provenance, safe failure, seeded variants and same-environment reproduction.
- E4: 48 kHz/24-bit WAV default, duration/channels, signal integrity, requested loop treatment and functional brief matching without perfect realism.
- E5: Portable static audition, version comparison and downloads, without synthesis expertise or a large dashboard.
- E6: Validated data/trusted synthesis boundary, no arbitrary model-authored shell/program execution, credential protection and bounded local resources.

Internal compiler, schema and store choices remain engineering hypotheses, not
ornamental contracts. CLI and static audition are distinct surfaces with the
explicit caller-contract grid; no MCP implementation is silently required.
